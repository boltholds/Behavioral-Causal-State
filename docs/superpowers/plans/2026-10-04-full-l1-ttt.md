- Resume preserves optimizer/RNG/early-stop state and verifies data/code/config hashes; budget does not restart silently.

- Confidence bounds use independent groups and declared strata; invalid output incurs1; core/simulator comparisons remain distinct.
- No pending human review, unavailable resource, incomplete seed, omitted rare estimand or failed gate can become a passed complete study.
